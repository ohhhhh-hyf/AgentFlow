## [Briefing Overview]
[In one paragraph, approx. 180–220 words: state the releasing entity, time and place, and the main purpose of the briefing; summarize the key business segments and overall development trend, and briefly describe the Q&A concerns and follow-up work arrangements; outline the full picture at a macro level and do not expand into details.]

## [Key Information]
[Use the validated agenda as the main thread: list each topic as a top-level heading in the form "### I. Topic Title"; within each subsection, objectively present the policy points, business measures, and quantitative results announced under that topic: for multi-direction or complex measures, divide the content into paragraphs with an independent bold line "**Business Sub-section / Measure Category**"; for a single direction, use a flat list. Start each body item with a substantive phrase that reflects the specific business action or outcome (e.g., "- **Measure / Outcome Point**: specific measures, progress, and core quantitative data"); naturally bold key data, and stop once the factual basis and work results are objectively explained.]

## [Official Statements]
[Comprehensively organize the authoritative tone, core principles, and policy direction of the spokesperson and official entities; for each item, start an independent line with "- **Statement Topic**" (add "(Name / Role)" when the spokesperson can be clearly identified), and add indented sub-items "  - " to list the core viewpoints and factual basis separately; quote key original remarks directly and in a standard way, without extra labels, and explain the position and basis clearly. Specific Q&A interactions are presented under "Q&A Session".]

## [Q&A Session]
[(Present only if there was interactive Q&A; adaptively omit this section entirely if there was no interactive Q&A.) Comprehensively distill the Q&A interaction, presenting one question and one answer as a pair: the question line "**Questioner**: core focus of the question" is immediately followed by the answer line "**Respondent**: clear conclusion and official policy position"; state the media institution or reporter identity on the question side (if not mentioned, write "**Q**"), and the specific spokesperson or official identity on the answer side (if not mentioned, write "**A**"); the answer should first state the official tone or conclusion in one sentence, then state the key factual basis and policy position.]
