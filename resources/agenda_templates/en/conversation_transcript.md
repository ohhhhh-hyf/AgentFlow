## [Conversation Overview]
[Single paragraph, approx. 150–250 words: state the identities and backgrounds of the participating parties, the motivation and occasion for the conversation, the mainline of the validated agenda topics, and the overall tendency and outcome; keep it at the macro level and leave specific interactions to "Discussion Content", consensus and disagreements to "Consensus & Disagreements", and follow-up thinking to "Follow-up Topics"; do not expand into details in this section.]

## [Discussion Content]
[Use the validated agenda as the main thread: list each topic as a top-level heading in the form "### I. Topic Title", with no introductory transition sentence; within each subsection, objectively present the in-depth exchange under that topic: for multi-direction or complex exchanges, divide the content into paragraphs with an independent bold line "**Discussion Sub-topic / Dimension**"; for a single direction, use a flat list. Start each body item with a substantive phrase that reflects the speaker or core viewpoint (e.g., "- **Speaker / Discussion Focus**: core claim, reasoning logic, and specific details"), concretely state specific statements, supporting cases, quantitative data, and interaction details, and stop once the essence of the exchange and the interaction logic are clear.]

## [Consensus & Disagreements]
[Present the certain conclusions and cognitive differences reached through the exchange in subsections: 1) "### Consensus Reached": list items one by one in the form "- **Consensus Focus**: the core conclusion, cooperation intention, and basis agreed by all"; 2) "### Key Disagreements": list items one by one in the form "- **Point of Disagreement**: different positions, concerns, and respective reasons of each party"; adaptively omit a subsection if there is no corresponding content.]

## [Follow-up Topics]
[List the follow-up agreements, action intentions, or open-ended thinking derived from the conversation as a checklist: list them item by item in the form "- **Derived Topic / Item**: specific trend, follow-up plan, and room for discussion"; clearly state the focus and follow-up intention; adaptively omit this section if there was no follow-up discussion.]
