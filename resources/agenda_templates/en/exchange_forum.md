## [Background]
[Single paragraph, approx. 60–90 words: state the institutions/identities of the participating parties, the reason for the exchange and the main objective, and the overall tone; lead with conclusions, outline the full picture at a macro level, and do not expand into specific topics or data details in this section.]

## [Key Information]
[Use the validated agenda as the main thread: list each topic as a top-level heading in the form "### I. Topic Title"; within each subsection, distill the key facts and objective data synchronized under that topic, strictly control length, and eliminate redundancy: for multi-direction or complex matters, divide the content into paragraphs with an independent bold line "**Business Sub-topic / Dimension**"; for a single direction, use a flat list. Start each body item with a substantive business phrase that reflects the specific business entity or fact point (e.g., "- **Business Entity / Fact Point**: specific facts and quantitative support"), lead with conclusions, merge similar items to distill key facts, involved milestones, and core metrics, keep each item approx. 25–50 words, and stop once the factual basis is clear; this section includes only objective facts and data, while subjective requests and post-meeting decisions are left to later sections.]

## [Stakeholder Requests]
[Record the core claims and concerns of multiple parties: list them by participating party in the form "- **Participating Institution / Identity**: core claims and requests (closely tied to expected actions and goals) and main concerns (difficulties or preconditions)"; go straight to the core of the requests and substantive disagreements; do not repeat background or data.]

## [Outcomes]
[Summarize the consensus decisions and follow-up arrangements explicitly confirmed by both parties on site, and present them directly as bullet items starting with "- " according to the actual discussion: state the specific conclusion, tone-setting principle, cooperation commitment, or follow-up action (including the responsible party and delivery deadline); one matter per item, and stop once the substantive action is clear; adaptively omit this section if there are no clear conclusions or arrangements.]
